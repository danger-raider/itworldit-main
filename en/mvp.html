Minimum Viable Product

The first product version that gives real answers

An MVP (Minimum Viable Product) is the first version of a product with the most important features. It helps test an idea in the market, collect user feedback and decide whether it is worth investing in full-scale development.

Why it matters

MVP helps you launch validated solutions, not assumptions

  • 01 Fast launch without spending budget on untested features
  • 02 Demand validation with real users
  • 03 A clear technical foundation for the next stages
  • 04 A working product for investors or partners

Who it is for

Who needs an MVP

An MVP is useful when you need to quickly understand whether an idea has demand, or whether it should be changed before investing a large budget into full development.

  • Startups that want to test an idea quickly
  • Businesses that want to launch a new product with lower risk
  • Entrepreneurs looking for investors who need a working prototype
