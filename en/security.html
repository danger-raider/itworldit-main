Email Protection

Email protection starts with the domain

SPF, DKIM and DMARC help mail services understand which messages are actually allowed to be sent on behalf of your domain. This does not replace common sense, but it strongly improves basic protection.

SPF who is allowed to send
DKIM whether the message was not altered
DMARC what to do with suspicious messages
