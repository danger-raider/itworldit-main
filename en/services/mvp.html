Minimum Viable Product

The first product version that gives real answers

An MVP (Minimum Viable Product) is the first version of a product with the most important features. It allows you to test an idea on the market, collect feedback from users and decide whether it is worth investing in full-scale development.

  • Fast launch without unnecessary costs for untested features
  • Demand validation with real users
  • A clear technical foundation for the next stages
  • The ability to show a working product to investors or partners
